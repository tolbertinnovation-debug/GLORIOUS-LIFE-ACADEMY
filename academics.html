STAGE 1 · EARLY CHILDHOOD

A joyful first step into learning.

Nursery (ABC)Kindergarten 1Kindergarten 2

Through songs, stories, play, and hands-on activities, our youngest learners discover letters, sounds, numbers, shapes, and colours while building confidence, good habits, and friendships.

  • Letters, phonics & early reading
  • Counting, numbers & shapes
  • Creative play, music & art
  • Listening, sharing & good manners

STAGE 2 · LOWER PRIMARY

Strong foundations for every subject.

Grade 1Grade 2Grade 3

Learners grow into confident readers, writers, and problem solvers, with caring teachers who build solid literacy and numeracy skills and a love of discovery.

  • Reading, writing & spelling
  • Arithmetic & number sense
  • Introduction to science & our world
  • Character, faith & teamwork

STAGE 3 · UPPER PRIMARY

Curious minds, growing independence.

Grade 4Grade 5Grade 6

Students deepen their knowledge across core subjects, learn to think critically, express ideas clearly, and take responsibility for their own learning.

  • Language arts & comprehension
  • Mathematics & reasoning
  • Science & social studies
  • Leadership, discipline & values

STAGE 4 · JUNIOR HIGH

Ready to soar to the next level.

Grade 7Grade 8Grade 9

Our junior high students build advanced academic skills, strong study habits, and the character and purpose needed for a confident transition to senior high school.

  • Advanced English & mathematics
  • General science & social studies
  • Study skills & exam readiness
  • Citizenship, integrity & purpose
