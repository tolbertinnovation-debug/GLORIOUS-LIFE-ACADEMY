HOW ADMISSIONS WORK

Three simple steps.

Joining Glorious Life Academy starts with a conversation.

  1. 1

    Send an enquiry

    Fill in the form below. It opens WhatsApp with your message ready to send to our office.

  2. 2

    Hear from our office

    We reply on WhatsApp with available places, requirements, important dates, and fees for your child’s class.

  3. 3

    Visit and enrol

    Visit the Academy, meet our team, and complete your child’s enrolment.

QUESTIONS PARENTS ASK

Good to know.

Which classes does Glorious Life Academy offer?+

We welcome learners from Nursery (ABC) and Kindergarten (K1, K2) through Grades 1 to 9. Explore our academics.

How do I find out about requirements, dates, and fees?+

Send an enquiry using the form on this page. Our office will reply on WhatsApp with the details for your child’s class.

Can I visit the Academy before enrolling?+

Yes. Choose “School visit” in the enquiry form and tell us when suits you.

What values guide the Academy?+

Discipline, Learning, Character, and Excellence, rooted in Godly values. Learn more about us.
