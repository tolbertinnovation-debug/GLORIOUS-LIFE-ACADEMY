WELCOME TO OUR ACADEMY

Education with values.
A future with purpose.

Glorious Life Academy is committed to providing quality, values-based education that develops the intellectual, moral, spiritual, and practical capacities of every learner.

Our leadership structure is designed to promote strong governance, academic excellence, accountability, transparency, and sustainable institutional growth.

The Academy is guided by a Board of Directors and an administrative leadership team whose combined experience, commitment, and oversight support the Academy’s mission and long-term vision.

GOVERNANCE & ACCOUNTABILITY

Strong foundations.
Responsible leadership.

Leadership responsibilities are clearly defined, with accountability, transparency, and the best interests of students at the center of institutional decisions.

01

Board oversight

The Board of Directors provides governance, policy oversight, strategic direction, and institutional accountability.

02

Institutional leadership

The Country Director/Founder and administrative leadership implement approved policies and manage the Academy’s operations.

03

Academic standards

The Principal/Education Director leads academic and educational functions, working closely with administrative leadership and teaching staff to maintain high educational standards.

04

Financial stewardship

Financial management is supported through the Accountant/CFO, with appropriate reporting and oversight mechanisms to promote responsible stewardship of the Academy’s resources.
