Board of Directors

01

Chairman, Board of Directors

Apostolic Bishop Michael O. Brokenborough

Responsibilities +

Provides overall governance and strategic oversight of the Academy. The Chairman provides leadership to the Board, supports institutional accountability, and offers guidance on the Academy’s long-term development and sustainability.

02

Co-Chairman, Board of Directors

Bishop Tubman Sarpee

Responsibilities +

Supports the Chairman in providing governance, strategic direction, and institutional oversight. The Co-Chairman also contributes to strengthening partnerships, leadership development, and the Academy’s continued growth.

03

Board Member

Pastor Daniel Bertin Johnson

Responsibilities +

Serves as a member of the Board of Directors, working under the leadership and guidance of the Co-Chairman in matters assigned by the Board. He contributes to governance, institutional development, community engagement, and the Academy’s strategic objectives.

04

Country Director / Founder

Apostle Michael Whea Mardeh

Responsibilities +

Provides executive and institutional leadership for Glorious Life Academy. As Founder and Country Director, he oversees the Academy’s strategic vision, development initiatives, external relations, partnerships, institutional expansion, and implementation of Board-approved policies.

05

Co-Founder / Research Analyst

Yassah Kowel Mardeh

Responsibilities +

Supports the Academy’s institutional development through research, analysis, planning, and evidence-based recommendations. She contributes to educational research, program development, institutional improvement, and the identification of opportunities that strengthen the Academy’s impact and effectiveness.

06

Principal / Education Director

Johnathan Tako Tarr

Responsibilities +

Leads the Academy’s academic and educational operations. The Principal oversees teaching and learning, staff coordination, curriculum implementation, student development, academic standards, and the day-to-day educational administration of the Academy.

07

Vice Principal for Administration

Emmanuel Y. M. Korsee

Responsibilities +

Supports the Principal in the effective administration and operation of the Academy. His responsibilities include administrative coordination, student affairs, staff support, institutional procedures, and other operational functions assigned by the Academy’s leadership.

08

Accountant / Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

Pastor Agnes W. Smith

Responsibilities +

Provides financial management and accountability for the Academy. The Accountant/CFO oversees financial records, budgeting, financial reporting, internal controls, and responsible stewardship of institutional resources in accordance with the Academy’s policies and applicable requirements.
